How to enter the collection

Four ideas are enough to navigate this museum. Then use the guided replay or take manual control.

01

A frame is a complete room

Its artifact, narrative, motif, timeline position, curator, and provenance are hashed together. Change one thing and its identity changes.

02

A fork is not a replacement

A curator can begin a new future from any verified room. The source history remains visitable, byte-for-byte.

03

Agreement may be merged

Compatible motifs can share a new room with both parents named. Contradictory interpretations keep separate galleries.

04

Corruption stops at first contact

If an old artifact is altered without new provenance, verification freezes that path at its last valid room. Accepted forks survive.

Scope

This is an explanatory, fully synthetic model of content-addressed cultural memory—not a claim about real museums, consensus, legal provenance, or long-term storage. SHA-256 checks are real; curators, dates, rooms, and histories are deterministic fiction.
